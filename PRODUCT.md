# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

wow-addon

## Users

WoW Forever players working through the Tier 0.5 (Dungeon Set 2) upgrade chain, installing from CurseForge. Most have never seen the original spreadsheet, so nothing can assume it. They open the window in two moments, equally often: at the Auction House during a buying session, and out in the world mid-farm to check how many Blackrock Bracers or Volcanic Ash are still needed. Either faction; the Alliance and Horde versions of the chain differ in one item.

## Product Purpose

Turn the T0.5 shopping list into an in-game checklist that counts what you carry, prices what you still need from Auctionator's last scan, and tells you how much gold is left to spend. Success is reaching every quest giver with everything in your bags and no surprise gold shortfall.

## Positioning

The whole T0.5 cost at a glance: every gold fee, AH mat and farm item for the Dungeon Set 2 chain, live-priced, in one list.

## Brand Personality

Native, calm, trustworthy. It should read as a panel Blizzard could have shipped: gold headers, the stock frame, game fonts. It informs without nagging and never competes with the game world for attention.

## Anti-references

TSM-style density: walls of tiny numbers, tabs and columns that turn the window into a spreadsheet. Generic web-app styling: flat modern cards and pills that clash with the game UI. Bright neon or saturated accents that shout over the game world.

## Design Principles

1. **Glanceable in two seconds.** The one number you came for (what's left to buy, or how many to farm) must be readable without scanning the row.
2. **Look built-in.** Use Blizzard's own frames, fonts and colors before inventing anything.
3. **Never mislead about price.** A live Auctionator price, a stale one, and a spreadsheet fallback must look different, every time.
4. **Done gets out of the way.** Finished rows drop to the bottom and quiet down, so what's left is what you see.
5. **Explain itself to a stranger.** A first-time user with no spreadsheet should understand each row and section without a tooltip.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Colorblind-safe: done versus missing and live versus fallback price are never signaled by color alone; each also carries a mark or text (a checkmark, "(sheet)", an age like "3d"). Text stays at or above the game's standard small fonts, with contrast against the frame background at 4.5:1 for row text.
